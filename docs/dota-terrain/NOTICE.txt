Dota 2 hero models, textures, animations and names are Valve Corporation's content.
Model catalog source: https://github.com/justkiddingxd/dota-loadout
The exact source revision is pinned in ../dota-models.json. Only the 10 selected
heroes are fetched by the browser, not the entire model collection.
No data or backend service from datdota/broodmother is used.

Map overview texture: OpenDota web repository
https://github.com/odota/web/blob/master/public/assets/images/dota2/map/detailed_740.webp
Terrain is a visual reconstruction based on map 7.40, not a live collision mesh.
Buildings and players are positioned from the Valve live match frame.
Decorative trees, rocks and lights are not live game entities.

This viewer is not affiliated with or endorsed by Valve.
